    • Field: digital technology
    • Track: Leader (Exceptional Talent). Mandatory criterion, recognised by others as:
    • Plus at least 2 optional criteria:
      • Innovation as a founder or senior executive of a product-led digital tech company
      • Innovation as an employee working on a new digital field or concept
      • Contributions to the sector outside your job (for example mentoring or collaborative projects)
      • Significant technical, commercial or entrepreneurial contributions at a product-led company
      • Published or expert-endorsed research
    Evidence needed

    3Recommendation letters

    • Each author is an established expert in digital technology who has known your work for at least 12 months.
    • Written for this Global Talent application, not reused from another purpose.
    • Must cover: how the author knows you; your achievements in digital technology and how you are a leader; how you would benefit from living in the UK; how you would contribute to UK digital technology; your future work plans.
    • Format: typed, dated and signed; up to 3 single sides of A4 (author credentials and contact details excluded); author phone and email, plus organisation logo and registered address if applicable; attach the author's CV or other proof of credentials.

    10Evidence documents (maximum)

    • At least 2 documents for the mandatory criterion (recognised as a leading talent).
    • At least 4 documents covering 2 of the optional criteria.
    • Each document up to 3 sides of A4.
    • One document cannot be used for more than one criterion.

    1CV

    • Typed, up to 3 sides of A4, covering your career and any relevant publication history.

      Letters, documents and CV as listed under Evidence needed.
    2. Stage 1: Endorsement5 to 8 weeks
      Apply on GOV.UK (£561). Tech Nation reviews it for the Home Office.
    3. Stage 2: VisaAbout 3 weeks
      Apply from outside the UK (£205) plus the healthcare surcharge (£1,035 per year). Earliest is 3 months before travel.
    4. Settlement (indefinite leave to remain)After 3 years
      Available after 3 years on the Leader track.
    Activation after approval
    Create a UKVI account, link your passport. No sticker or card: the eVisa is your status.Same day
    ID: none issued. You prove status with an eVisa share code.Instant

    • Spouse PR after 5 yrs, not 3.
