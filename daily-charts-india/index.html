Oct 8, 2026 · Hidden urbanization

India's official count puts 36% of people in towns and cities. Satellite data puts it at 84%, about 700 million more urban residents.

Share of population living in urban areas in 2025 for the 30 most populous countries, by each country's own definition and by the same satellite-based measure for every country. Sorted by the gap.

Note: The satellite measure (the UN-endorsed Degree of Urbanisation) counts anyone living in a city, town or suburb, using a 1 km grid of built-up area and population. India's own definition requires a town to have 5,000 people, 400 people per km² and 75% of male workers outside farming, and towns run by village councils stay administratively rural. 2025 values are estimates. Data: European Commission JRC (2025), UN World Urbanization Prospects (2025), both via Our World in Data.

Oct 8, 2026 · India's income since the Mughals

In 1600 the average Indian earned three quarters as much as the average Briton. At independence in 1947, it was less than a tenth.

India's GDP per person as a share of Britain's, in inflation-adjusted dollars at 2011 prices. India's own income per person was lower in 1947 ($985) than in 1600 ($1,264).

Note: Figures before 1870 are reconstructions from historical wages, prices and farm output, and carry wide error margins. Gaps between points before 1884 are years with no estimate. Data: Maddison Project Database 2023 (Bolt and van Zanden), with India before 1871 from Broadberry, Custodis and Gupta (2015).
