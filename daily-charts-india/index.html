Oct 8, 2026 · India's income since the Mughals

In 1600 the average Indian earned three quarters as much as the average Briton. At independence in 1947, it was less than a tenth.

India's GDP per person as a share of Britain's, in inflation-adjusted dollars at 2011 prices. India's own income per person was lower in 1947 ($985) than in 1600 ($1,264).

Note: Figures before 1870 are reconstructions from historical wages, prices and farm output, and carry wide error margins. Gaps between points before 1884 are years with no estimate. Data: Maddison Project Database 2023 (Bolt and van Zanden), with India before 1871 from Broadberry, Custodis and Gupta (2015).
