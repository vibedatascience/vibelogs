Oct 8, 2026 · Mughal nobility

By Aurangzeb's last decades, almost 1 in 3 top Mughal nobles was Hindu, and more than half of them were Marathas

Each square is one noble holding a rank (mansab) of 1,000 or more. Rows are periods. Gray squares are all other nobles, mostly Iranis, Turanis, Afghans and Indian Muslims.

Note: Counts differ by source and by the rank cutoff used. The Maratha count is shown only for 1679 to 1707, where it is published separately. Data: M. Athar Ali, The Mughal Nobility under Aurangzeb (Oxford University Press), as reproduced in Nowgong Girls' College notes and SelfStudyHistory.
