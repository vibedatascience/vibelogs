The five rules

Read once
  1. Write down every weightPhone notes is fine. Next time you do that exercise, beat it by one rep or a little weight. If the numbers never move, your body has no reason to change.
  2. Eat about 1,850 caloriesYour maintenance is around 2,300. This puts you at roughly one pound of fat a week. Going lower does not speed it up, it just costs you muscle.
  3. Protein at every mealAim for 140 to 155 grams a day. A palm sized portion of chicken, fish, eggs, paneer, or greek yogurt, four times a day, gets you most of the way.
  4. Three or four drinks a week, none on lifting daysYou do not have to quit. Beer is roughly 180 calories each and it wrecks the sleep you need to recover.
  5. Weigh daily, judge weeklySame time each morning, before eating. Only look at the seven day average. Day to day swings of two or three pounds are water and mean nothing.
