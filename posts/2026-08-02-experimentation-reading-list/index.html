Experimentation reading list

Nine books on experimentation and A/B testing, saved from a LinkedIn reading list. I looked up the table of contents for each so I can decide which ones to read and where to dip in.

1. Experimentation Works

Stefan H. Thomke, 2020

Experimentation as a management capability, not a testing technique. Written for leaders who want to scale learning instead of opinions.

  1. Why Experimentation Works
  2. What Makes a Good Business Experiment?
  3. How to Experiment Online
  4. Can Your Culture Handle Large-Scale Experimentation?
  5. Inside an Experimentation Organization
  6. Becoming an Experimentation Organization

2. Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments

Ron Kohavi, Diane Tang, Ya Xu, 2020

The gold standard for running experiments correctly at scale. Five parts, 23 chapters. Companion site: experimentguide.com.

Part I: Introductory topics for everyone

  1. Introduction and Motivation
  2. Running and Analyzing Experiments: An End-to-End Example
  3. Twyman's Law and Experimentation Trustworthiness
  4. Experimentation Platform and Culture

Part II: Selected topics for everyone

  1. Speed Matters: An End-to-End Case Study
  2. Organizational Metrics
  3. Metrics for Experimentation and the Overall Evaluation Criterion
  4. Institutional Memory and Meta-Analysis
  5. Ethics in Controlled Experiments

Part III: Complementary and alternative techniques

  1. Complementary Techniques
  2. Observational Causal Studies

Part IV: Advanced topics for building a platform

  1. Client-Side Experiments
  2. Instrumentation
  3. Choosing a Randomization Unit
  4. Ramping Experiment Exposure: Trading Off Speed, Quality, and Risk
  5. Scaling Experiment Analyses

Part V: Advanced topics for analyzing experiments

  1. The Statistics Behind Online Controlled Experiments
  2. Variance Estimation and Improved Sensitivity: Pitfalls and Solutions
  3. The A/A Test
  4. Triggering for Improved Sensitivity
  5. Sample Ratio Mismatch and Other Trust-Related Guardrail Metrics
  6. Leakage and Interference Between Variants
  7. Measuring Long-Term Treatment Effects

3. Design for Impact

Erin Weigel, 2024

Bridges UX design and experimentation through her Conversion Design process. The chapters follow the phases of that process.

  1. Conversion Design Drives Impact
  2. The Understand Phase: Uncovering Impactful Insights
  3. The Hypothesize Phase: Think Clear, Logical Thoughts
  4. The Prioritize Phase: The Work and the Workflow
  5. The Create Phase: Set Your Idea Up for Success
  6. The Test Phase: Test Like You're Wrong
  7. The Analyze Phase: Learn from the Data
  8. The Decide Phase: Make an Optimal Choice
  9. Scale It: Drive Impact Across Your Organization

4. Statistical Methods in Online A/B Testing

Georgi Z. Georgiev, 2019

The statistics behind experiments, built from the ground up with CRO examples instead of dice and coins.

  1. Using Statistics in Business
  2. Estimating Uncertainty: Statistical Significance, P-Values, Other Estimates
  3. Statistical Assumptions: Assessing Model Adequacy
  4. Statistical Power and Sample Size Calculations
  5. Types of Statistical Hypotheses
  6. Tests with More Than One Variant
  7. Segmentation and Multiple Performance Indicators
  8. Working with Continuous Data
  9. Percentage Change
  10. Sequential Testing: Continuous Monitoring of Data
  11. Optimal Significance Thresholds and Sample Sizes
  12. External Validity, a.k.a. Generalizability of A/B Test Results
  13. Miscellaneous Topics
  14. Communicating Statistical Results

5. Tiny Experiments

Anne-Laure Le Cunff, 2025

A mindset book about learning through small, intentional experiments in your own life. Organized in four parts: Pact, Act, React, Impact.

Pact: commit to curiosity

  1. Why Goal Setting Is Broken
  2. Escaping the Tyranny of Purpose
  3. A Pact to Turn Doubts into Experiments

Act: practice mindful productivity

  1. A Deeper Sense of Time
  2. Procrastination Is Not the Enemy
  3. The Power of Intentional Imperfection

React: collaborate with uncertainty

  1. Creating Growth Loops
  2. The Secret to Better Decisions
  3. How to Dance with Disruption

Impact: grow with the world

  1. How to Unlock Social Flow
  2. Learning in Public
  3. Life Beyond Legacy

6. Next-Level A/B Testing

Leemay Nassery, 2025

What breaks when experimentation matures: throughput, interaction effects, ML evaluation, long-term impact. From a Pragmatic Bookshelf engineering angle.

  1. Why Experimentation Rate, Quality, and Cost Matter
  2. Improving Experimentation Throughput
  3. Designing Better Experiments
  4. Improving Machine Learning Evaluation Practices
  5. Verifying and Monitoring Experiments
  6. Ensuring Trustworthy Insights
  7. Practicing Adaptive Testing Strategies
  8. Measuring Long-Term Impact
  9. Tying It All Together

7. You Should Test That

Chris Goward, 2013

A classic on hypothesis-driven conversion optimization, built around his LIFT model.

  1. Why You Should Test That
  2. What Is Conversion Optimization?
  3. Prioritize Testing Opportunities
  4. Create Hypotheses with the LIFT Model
  5. Optimize Your Value Proposition
  6. Optimize for Relevance
  7. Optimize for Clarity
  8. Optimize for Anxiety
  9. Optimize for Distraction
  10. Optimize for Urgency
  11. Test Your Hypotheses
  12. Analyze Your Test Results
  13. Strategic Marketing Optimization

8. Growth Data Analytics Playbook

Mengying Li, Joe Kumar, Yuzheng Sun, 2025

Connects experimentation, metrics, and decision-making for product growth. Published by Statsig.

  1. Leverage Growth Analytics to Drive Product Success
  2. Identify Early Signals of Product Value and Success
  3. Build the Foundation with Growth Accounting Framework
  4. Acquire and Foster High-Quality Users
  5. Retain Existing Users and Keep Them Engaged
  6. Resurrect Churned Customers: Strategies for Reengagement
  7. Accelerate User Conversion for Increased Revenue Generation
  8. Optimize Performance and Build Sustainable Growth Flywheels
  9. Size Growth Opportunity and Set Achievable Growth Goals
  10. Design and Implement Effective Experiments

9. Thinking in Bets

Annie Duke, 2018

Not about A/B testing directly. It is about decision-making under uncertainty, which every experiment ultimately is.

  1. Life Is Poker, Not Chess
  2. Wanna Bet?
  3. Bet to Learn: Fielding the Unfolding Future
  4. The Buddy System
  5. Dissent to Win
  6. Adventures in Mental Time Travel
