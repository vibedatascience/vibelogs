USA Treasury bill: a primer

A Treasury bill is a short loan to the US government. You pay a bit less than face value today and get the full amount back in 4 to 52 weeks. The difference is your interest.

Buying and taxes

Where
TreasuryDirect from $100, any brokerage, or a money market fund that holds bills.
Auctions
Most terms every week, 52-week every four. Bid non-competitively to take the auction rate.
Taxes
Federal income tax, yes. State and local income tax, no.
