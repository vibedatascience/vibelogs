USA Treasuries: bills, notes and bonds

A Treasury is a loan to the US government. Bills last up to a year, notes 2 to 10 years, bonds 20 or 30. Same borrower, same promise. They differ in how long you wait and how you get paid.

Three kinds

termshow it pays
Bill4, 6, 8, 13, 17, 26, 52 wkBought below face value. No interest checks; the discount is the interest.
Note2, 3, 5, 7, 10 yrA fixed coupon every 6 months, then face value at the end.
Bond20, 30 yrSame as a note, just longer.

What $1,000 pays you

Every payment over the life of each, bought today at today's yields.

Small ticks are coupons every 6 months. Tall bar is the $1,000 back at the end.
