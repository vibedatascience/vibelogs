Notes and method
  • Stages follow the Administrative Procedure Act. Stage 0 is a catalog entry, not a legal step. Courts can only review a final rule.
  • Projections are offsets from the comment close date. Fast (weighted lottery): OIRA at 45 d, final at 66 d, effective at 126 d. Slow (D/S): OIRA at 218 d, cleared at 261 d, final at 291 d, effective at 351 d.
  • OIRA clearance is an internal sign-off, not a legal check. D/S cleared both reviews and was still blocked. OIRA time on proposals ranged from 0 d (cap fee) to 62 d (DOL wage).
  • The Unified Agenda lags. The grace period rule reached OIRA before it was listed, and the OPT fee was never listed.
  • No reginfo OIRA record was found for the wage, grace period and biometrics proposals. The first two dates come from law firm alerts.
  • Not rules, so not in the table: USCIS biometrics appointments for most I-765s, incl. H-4 EAD, since Dec 12, 2025; Edakunni bundling ended Jan 18, 2025; the 540-day EAD auto-extension ended for renewals filed on or after Oct 30, 2025.
  • Also not tracked: the $100k H-1B proclamation (vacated Jun 8, 2026, on appeal at the First Circuit), the EAD auto-extension IFR, HR-1 statutory fees, and the withdrawn EB-1/NIW reforms rule (1615-AC85).
  • D/S case: Presidents' Alliance v. DHS, No. 1:26-cv-13799 (D. Mass.). Dkt 50 granted APA § 705 postponement; dkt 51 applies it nationwide.
