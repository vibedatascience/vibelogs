Notes

  • Every rule on this page goes through the same nine stages under the Administrative Procedure Act. Stage 0 is a catalog entry, not a legal step. Stages 1 and 5 are the two White House reviews. Court review needs a final rule and a plaintiff; nobody can sue over a proposal.
  • Expected dates are offsets from the comment close date, taken from the two finished DHS rules. Fast track (weighted lottery): sent to OIRA about 45 days after close, final rule at 66 days, effective at 126 days. Slow track (D/S): sent to OIRA at 218 days, cleared at 261, final at 291, effective at 351. Where the fast date has already passed the cell says "any day."
  • OIRA clearance is an internal sign-off, not a legal check. D/S cleared both reviews and was still blocked. OIRA time on the proposal ranged from 0 days (cap fee, in and out the same day) to 62 days (DOL wage).
  • The Unified Agenda has been published after the fact. The "Spring 2025" edition came out Sep 4, 2025, a week after the D/S proposal was already in the Federal Register. The grace period rule reached OIRA eight days before it first appeared on any agenda. The OPT fee never appeared at all.
  • Three OIRA review records could not be located on reginfo: DOL wage (proposal), grace period (proposal), biometrics (proposal). The dates shown for the first two come from law firm alerts that cite reginfo; the biometrics cell is left as "dates not captured."
  • Practice on the ground that is not a rule and so not in the table: since Dec 12, 2025 USCIS no longer accepts self-submitted photos and schedules a biometrics appointment for most I-765 filings, including H-4 EAD; the Edakunni settlement that required H-4 and H-4 EAD filings to be adjudicated with the premium-processed H-1B expired Jan 18, 2025, so bundling is now discretionary; the 540-day automatic EAD extension ended for renewals filed on or after Oct 30, 2025.
  • Not tracked here: the $100k H-1B proclamation (an executive action, not a rule; vacated by D. Mass. on Jun 8, 2026, appeal pending at the First Circuit); the EAD auto-extension interim final rule (took effect the day it published, no comment period beforehand); the HR-1 fee codification (statutory fees, already in force); the EB-1/NIW "Petition for Immigrant Worker Reforms" rule (1615-AC85, withdrawn from the agenda Aug 14, 2026).
  • D/S litigation: Presidents' Alliance v. DHS, No. 1:26-cv-13799 (D. Mass.). Docket 50 is the memorandum and order granting APA § 705 postponement and denying vacatur without prejudice; docket 51 is the order implementing it nationwide. Status conference Oct 2, 2026.
