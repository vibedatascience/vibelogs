Dates for finished stages come from reginfo.gov (the OIRA review log) and the Federal Register. Expected dates assume the D/S rule's pace, about ten months from comment close to final rule.

Notes

  • OIRA touches a rule twice, at stage 2 and stage 6. Clearing OIRA is an internal sign-off and says nothing about whether a court will uphold the rule. The D/S rule cleared both passes and was still postponed.
  • OIRA time on the proposal: D/S 41 days, grace period 21 days, cap fee 0 days (received and concluded the same day).
  • Court review is gated at stage 7. Nobody can sue over a proposed rule.
  • The agenda has been trailing the rules. The "Spring 2025" agenda came out Sep 4, 2025, a week after the D/S proposal was already published. The grace period rule went to OIRA on Aug 6, 2026, eight days before it first appeared on an agenda.
  • D/S was blocked in Presidents' Alliance v. DHS, No. 1:26-cv-13799 (D. Mass.). The court found DHS failed to comply with the APA and postponed the effective date under 5 U.S.C. 705 pending review.
