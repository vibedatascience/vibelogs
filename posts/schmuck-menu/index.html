COCKTAIL 101every drink here is built from four parts.
spiritmodifierfruit & souraccent
the spirit is the backbone. the modifier softens or lengthens it (vermouth, sherry, cream, fizz). fruit & sour brings brightness. the accent is the twist: herbs, spice, fat, cheese.
